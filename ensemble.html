RECOMMENDED WORKING BUDGET

Three tiers, one recommendation.

MINIMUM CREDIBLE PROTOTYPE

$25,000–$35,000

Fashion shell, basic leather corset, simplified duster, riding trouser prototype, existing-platform trainer. No dedicated outsole tooling, no certification claim.

SERIOUS FUNCTIONAL PROTOTYPE

$35,000–$60,000

Protective riding base, couture outer modules, footwear/spat prototype, fittings, movement testing, revised tech packs, preliminary certification pathway.

PRODUCT-READY DROP 0

$75,000–$140,000+

Graded size range, size-set samples, dedicated footwear development, tooling decisions, repeatable BOM, QC standards, packaging, replacement parts, pre-production testing, initial production deposits.

All tiers exclude substantial inventory, freight, duty, certification-lab fees, and possible legal or insurance costs. Drop 0 figures assume the earlier route estimates hold; nothing is quoted.

Certification correction. Using D3O® does not automatically make the garment CE-certified. D3O’s motorcycle guidance distinguishes EN 1621 (armor components), EN 17092 (complete motorcycle garments) and EN 13634 (motorcycle footwear). The complete outfit needs its own assessment. Until then, describe a runway version as “armor-compatible performance fashion” — and only call it CE-certified after the complete garment has passed the relevant testing.

Recommendation. Do not begin with a Marvel-scale costume budget or a complete Drop 0. Start with the $35,000–$60,000 functional prototype program: the protective base specialist develops the chassis, the couture partner develops the shell, the footwear partner develops the trainer chassis, a qualified laboratory reviews the safety pathway, and only then decide whether custom outsole tooling and production grading are justified. The disciplined sequence: existing trainer platform → one fitted protective base → couture shell → movement testing → revised prototype → tooling decision → Drop 0.

The four-module specification.

Runway functionality notes

Riding and wind deflection. The split duster carries concealed magnetic hem weights that keep the train streamlined behind the rear wheel or stage fans without tangling in the chain or wheel spokes.

Dynamic range of motion. The corseted bodice uses floating leather side-gussets over high-tensile power-mesh, allowing full upper-body rotation and deep breathing while maintaining the waistline.

Modular tearaway mechanics. The duster unclips via two internal magnetic Fidlock shoulder anchors, transitioning from dramatic outdoor trench silhouette to a lightweight stage-ready corset-and-spats performance set in under three seconds.
