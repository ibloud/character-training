01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

A bond that can
change shape.

This work explores how PIXIE and her companion remain recognizable through relationship, symbols, and choice—not one fixed silhouette. The graduate-student framing updates this treatment without renaming the wider game’s Death and Rebirth pieces.

01 / NARRATIVE

Agency at the center

The philosophy graduate student questions how meaning and authority are assigned. Reading and reflection become choices she must make for herself.

02 / CHARACTER

Presence before form

PIXIE prefers an energetic state. When a form is useful, transportation is her preference; the human avatar is one optional way to connect.

03 / DESIGN

Visual continuity

A shared palette, sigil and inscription language connect distinct characters and manifestations while leaving room for the design to evolve.

02 / CREATIVE APPROACH

Story first.
Build with intent.

Move from a clear character relationship to visual anchors and practical production questions, so future modeling and performance choices have a reason behind them.

  1. 01

    Define the relationship

    Set the emotional contrast and the rules that keep PIXIE’s identity clear across forms.

  2. 02

    Find the visual anchors

    Explore the paired portrait, sigil, costume language, and the optional transportation manifestation.

  3. 03

    Plan beyond the still image

    Map what a future model, rig, animation, and authorized game integration would need to prove.
